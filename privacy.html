Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Waqty app for iPhone and this website. Waqty is run by Abdulazeez Adeleke Balogun, trading as Waqty Labs ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Waqty has no accounts, and the app never asks for your name or email.
  • Your settings and prayer times stay on your iPhone.
  • Your location is used to work out prayer times and weather. We don't receive or store it.
  • There are no ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information the app uses

Location

With your permission, Waqty uses your precise location while you're using the app. It's used to calculate prayer times for where you are, notice when you've travelled, and show local weather. Your last location is saved on your iPhone. To provide these features, your coordinates are sent directly from your iPhone to:

  • AlAdhan, which returns prayer times.
  • Open-Meteo, which returns the weather.

Those requests don't include your name or any account details. Like any web service, these providers can also see your IP address. You can turn off location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Prayer alarms and reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. We also use OneSignal to send push notifications and in-app messages, such as news about the app. When you use Waqty, OneSignal receives:

  • a push token and a OneSignal ID, which identify your device but not you by name
  • device details: model, iOS version, language, time zone, country, mobile carrier and app version
  • your IP address
  • whether notifications are allowed, and usage details such as when and how long you use the app

We don't share your GPS location with OneSignal. You can turn off notifications in iOS Settings, and deleting the app stops this collection.

App content and updates

Waqty downloads its What's New content from our Supabase backend and checks Apple's App Store for new versions. These requests don't include personal details, though the services can see your IP address as part of handling them.

Information stored only on your iPhone

Your settings, alarm choices, cached prayer times and widget data stay on your device and aren't sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Religious belief

We don't ask for your religion or keep a record of it, and the app doesn't send us information about your prayers. Using a prayer times app could suggest something about your beliefs, and we don't use your data to draw that conclusion.

Links that open outside the app

Coming Soon, Suggest a Feature and the Help Center open in Safari on a site run by Featurebase. Anything you post there is covered by Featurebase's own terms and may be visible to other people. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply.

Our website and waitlist

  • Waitlist: if you join, we store your email address with Supabase and use Resend to send you early access and launch emails. We keep it until you ask us to remove it or we no longer need it for those emails.
  • Site statistics: we use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies. The site is hosted by Vercel.
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see your IP address.

How we use information

We use it to provide the features above, deliver notifications, understand in general how the app is used, and keep it working. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell it. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (location, notifications and the waitlist) and on our legitimate interest in running and improving Waqty.

Keeping and sharing information

We share information only with the service providers named in this policy, or when the law requires it. These providers may process data in other countries, and each keeps it according to its own policy. We keep what we hold only for as long as we need it for the purposes above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or limit how it's used, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, contact us at the address below. Because the app has no accounts, we may need details from you to find any data related to your device.

Children

Waqty is suitable for all ages but isn't designed specifically for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal information, such as by joining the waitlist, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@getwaqty.app.
